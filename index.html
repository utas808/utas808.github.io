E Komo Mai! Welcome to Hawai'i

Hawai'i is a place with a interesting history, unique traditions, and a language that carries the knowledge of generations. From the first Polynesian voyagers to the Hawaiian Kingdom and the present day, the history of Hawai'i continues to shape the lives of its people.

This website explores important historical events, Native Hawaiian culture, ʻŌlelo Hawai'i, and the relationship between the Hawaiian people and the ʻāina. The goal is to help visitors learn about Hawai'i's past and understand why preserving its history and culture matters today.

Why Hawaiian History Matters

Learn Hawaiian history helps us understand the experiences, values, and knowledge of Natuve Hawaiians. History is more than remembering dates. It also includes understanding the stores, language, and traditions passed down through families and communities.

By learning about Hawai'i's past and supporting the preservation of ʻŌlelo Hawai'i, we can help ensure that Hawaiian culture continues to be respected, shared, and celebrated by future generations.
